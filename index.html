About

I am an incoming PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota. I recently completed my undergraduate study in Artificial Intelligence at Fudan University and am currently an intern at ByteDance Seed.

My research interests center on large language models, with a recent focus on interpretability and safety-related topics. I am also interested in efficient reasoning, test-time scaling, and LLM-based agents.

Research Interests

  • Interpretability and safety of large language models
  • LLM reasoning, efficient inference, and test-time scaling
  • LLM agents for information extraction and creative generation
  • Auto-formalization and mathematical reasoning
