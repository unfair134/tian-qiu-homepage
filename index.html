About

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Fudan University, broadly interested in large language models, reasoning, and agent systems.

My recent research explores efficient reasoning, test-time scaling, mathematical formalization, and LLM-based agents for information extraction and creative generation.

Research Interests

  • Large language model reasoning and efficient inference
  • Test-time scaling and reward-guided search
  • LLM agents for information extraction and creative generation
  • Auto-formalization and mathematical reasoning
